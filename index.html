Our 2064 Platform

1. Pragmatic Immigration

We believe in a sensible system prioritizing Caprica's economic needs by streamlining entry for non-CU laborers and graduates. Furthermore, we will always welcome asylum seekers fleeing tyranny with humanity and basic decency.

2. Sovereign Foreign Policy

Maintaining bilateral ties with global powers on grounds of goodwill, not subservience. We will expand military cooperation with the Columbian Union to establish a bulwark against authoritarianism and revanchist expansion.

3. Bold Climate Action

No new fossil fuel projects. We will champion ultra-low emission zones, heavily invest in renewable energy diversification, and launch massive national recycling and green electronics initiatives to end environmental destruction.

4. Multi-Domain Defense

Securing Caprica's future by introducing the Office of Reconnaissance for extraterrestrial intelligence, and the Office For Electronic Engagements for cyber defense, funded by a military-backed higher education program for Caprican youth.

5. An Equitable Economy

Ending austerity through Keynesian economics. We propose higher marginal tax brackets for the top percentiles, cutting VAT on essentials to 15%, establishing a State Co-operative Development Fund, and repealing deregulation acts like the CMA and CEMA to protect public assets.
