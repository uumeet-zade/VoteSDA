Manifesto • 2068

The Equitable Economy

Building a robust Keynesian framework that protects public assets, strengthens co-operative development, and ensures the wealth of Caprica works for the masses, not just the few.

Multi-Domain Defense

Modernizing our national security with advanced cyber capabilities and a sovereign defense posture, ensuring Caprica remains resilient in a rapidly changing world.

Sustainable Future

Transitioning aggressively to renewable energy sources, expanding ultra-low emission zones, and creating green jobs to secure a thriving environment for the next generation.

Pragmatic Immigration

We believe in a sensible system prioritizing Caprica's economic needs by streamlining entry for non-CU laborers and graduates, while welcoming asylum seekers fleeing tyranny with humanity and basic decency.
