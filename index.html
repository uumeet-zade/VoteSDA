The Coalition

Social Democratic Alliance

Driven by the principles of social democracy and progressivism, the SDA stands firmly against trickle-down economics and austerity. We advocate for a strong, centralized Columbian union, Keynesian economics, and the solidaristic vision of securing the full fruits of collective industry for the masses.

Rejuvenation

Guided by Yuan Kew Thought and regional developmentalism, Rejuvenation champions a socialist market economy. We are dedicated to anti-corruption governance, empowering regional autonomy, and driving massive long-term investments into public housing and social infrastructure.

Our 2064 Platform

1. A Socialist Market Economy

Rejecting neoliberalism and austerity, we commit to progressive taxation and Keynesian investment. We will balance common ownership and market socialism to ensure working Capricans reap the full fruits of their collective labor.

2. Sweeping Social Infrastructure & Housing

Implementing long-term economic planning to aggressively expand public housing and vital social infrastructure, ensuring developmentalist growth reaches every region of Caprica.

3. Transparent & Unified Governance

Bridging the SDA's vision for a strong, centralized Columbian state with Rejuvenation's dedication to regional autonomy and fierce anti-corruption measures to deliver a modern, accountable government.
